Potential Medical Data Privacy Class Action

Investigation Concerning ScriptWell

A potential class action is being investigated concerning allegations that ScriptWell collected, transmitted, disclosed, and/or sold certain information associated with individuals who used its website, mobile application, patient portal, or related digital services.

The investigation concerns whether ScriptWell permitted advertising, analytics, tracking, marketing, or other third-party technologies to receive information associated with individuals seeking or receiving medical services without providing adequate notice or obtaining legally sufficient authorization.

Information potentially involved may include medical searches, physician searches, treatment interests, appointment activity, health-related webpage activity, device information, IP addresses, advertising identifiers, browser identifiers, cookies, and other information capable of being associated with a particular user, device, or household.

Eligibility Question

Have you used ScriptWell's website, application, patient portal, or another ScriptWell digital service?

Information Potentially Transmitted

The investigation concerns tracking technologies that may have operated when users opened pages, searched for medical information, selected providers, reviewed treatments, scheduled appointments, completed forms, or otherwise interacted with ScriptWell's services.

When these interactions occurred, information may have been transmitted from the user's browser or device to third-party companies.

Potentially transmitted information may include:

Medical or treatment-related pages viewed, physician or specialist searches, medical conditions researched, treatment or procedure interests, appointment-related actions, URLs, page titles, form interactions, timestamps, IP addresses, device information, cookie identifiers, advertising IDs, browser characteristics, referral information, analytics identifiers, and other digital interaction data.

Certain technical identifiers may appear harmless when viewed independently. However, when combined with information already maintained by advertising platforms, analytics providers, or data companies, those identifiers may potentially be connected to a known user, account, device, or household.

Eligibility Question

Did you use ScriptWell to search for medical information, review a condition or treatment, find a provider, schedule an appointment, or access other health-related services?

Alleged Third-Party Data Sharing

The investigation seeks to determine whether ScriptWell incorporated third-party tracking technologies into health-related portions of its digital services.

These technologies may have allowed external companies to receive information about user activity for purposes such as analytics, advertising measurement, audience development, marketing optimization, conversion tracking, profiling, or targeted advertising.

For example, visiting a webpage concerning a particular medical condition, physician, treatment, medication, specialty, or procedure may generate an event containing information describing the page or interaction.

When combined with persistent identifiers, these events may potentially allow third parties to associate health-related browsing behavior with broader advertising or consumer profiles.

The investigation concerns:

Whether these transmissions occurred;

What information was transmitted;

Which companies received the information;

Whether information was sold, exchanged, or otherwise commercially utilized;

Whether users received adequate disclosure;

Whether users provided meaningful consent;

Whether information was retained or combined with existing consumer profiles;

And whether applicable privacy or consumer-protection laws were violated.

Eligibility Question

Before using ScriptWell, were you clearly informed that information about your health-related activity could be transmitted to advertising, analytics, marketing, or other third-party companies?

Why This Information May Be Sensitive

Health-related online activity can reveal significant information about an individual.

A person's interactions with a healthcare platform could potentially indicate interest in particular conditions, medications, procedures, physicians, specialists, diagnoses, treatments, reproductive-health services, mental-health services, or other medical matters.

The investigation concerns whether information associated with these activities was transmitted outside ScriptWell and whether users reasonably understood the extent of those transmissions.

It also seeks to determine whether users were provided a meaningful opportunity to decline or restrict this activity.

Eligibility Question

Did you specifically authorize ScriptWell to sell or disclose information concerning your health-related activity to third parties for advertising, marketing, profiling, or similar purposes?

Potential Legal Claims

Depending on the evidence obtained and the jurisdiction involved, potential claims may arise under applicable privacy laws, consumer-protection statutes, communications or interception laws, confidentiality protections, contractual obligations, unjust-enrichment principles, and other state or federal laws.

Whether a particular individual possesses a claim can depend on several factors, including:

The information transmitted;

The technologies involved;

The recipient of the information;

How the information was subsequently used;

Consent or authorization provided by the user;

The individual's state of residence;

And the specific privacy laws applicable to the conduct.

Potential Class

If litigation proceeds and a court ultimately certifies a class, the affected group could potentially include individuals who used ScriptWell's digital services during the relevant period and whose information was transmitted or disclosed to third parties through tracking, analytics, advertising, or similar technologies.

The precise class definition would depend upon evidence obtained during the investigation and any subsequent court proceedings.

Potential Compensation and Other Relief

Depending upon the claims asserted and applicable law, potential relief could include monetary compensation, statutory damages where authorized, restitution, disgorgement of certain profits, deletion of improperly obtained information, restrictions on future data collection or disclosure, changes to tracking practices, enhanced privacy protections, improved consent mechanisms, and attorneys' fees where permitted by law.

Participation in an investigation does not guarantee that litigation will be filed or that any particular individual will receive compensation.

Investigation of ScriptWell's Data Practices

A central issue in the investigation is determining what happened to information after it left a user's browser or device.

Investigators may evaluate network transmissions, tracking configurations, privacy disclosures, consent interfaces, agreements with third parties, data-retention practices, advertising systems, analytics systems, and related technical records.

The presence of third-party technology alone does not establish that confidential medical information was improperly disclosed.

The contents and context of the actual transmissions must be evaluated.

Records That May Be Relevant

Individuals who believe they may have been affected may wish to retain documentation showing their interactions with ScriptWell.

Potentially relevant materials could include appointment confirmations, account records, emails, privacy notices, consent screens, screenshots, communications received from ScriptWell, browser history, and other records demonstrating use of the relevant website or application.

Medical records, Social Security numbers, passwords, banking credentials, or unrelated sensitive documentation should not be submitted through an initial eligibility questionnaire.

What Happens After Submission

Responses may be reviewed to determine whether an individual's circumstances fall within the scope of the potential class action.

Additional information may be requested when necessary to evaluate whether the individual was potentially affected.

Providing information does not guarantee representation, compensation, settlement eligibility, or certification of a class.

The investigation may ultimately determine that certain users, transactions, technologies, or categories of information are not covered.

Potential Resolution

If claims ultimately proceed, potential outcomes could include litigation, negotiated resolution, changes to ScriptWell's privacy practices, restrictions on particular tracking technologies, deletion of certain information, monetary relief for qualifying individuals, or other remedies permitted by applicable law.

Any class settlement would ordinarily require court approval before compensation or other class-wide relief could be distributed.
